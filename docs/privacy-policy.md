# GoalStat Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 29, 2026_

GoalStat is a personal goal tracker. This policy explains what happens to the
information you enter into the app.

## What the app stores

The goals you create and the values you log for them (for example, "Read: yes"
or "Cardio: 45 min") are stored **only on your device**, in the app's private
storage.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** GoalStat has no account system, no analytics, and no servers. The
app does not send your goals, entries, or any other personal information to us
or to anyone else.

## Sharing

Your data leaves your device only when you choose to export it. The History
tab's export creates a CSV file and opens your device's share sheet; where that
file goes (email, cloud storage, and so on) is up to you and is governed by the
app you share it with.

## Backups

Android may include the app's data in your device's backup (for example, to
your Google account) if you have device backup turned on. That backup is
managed by Android and Google, not by GoalStat.

## Deleting your data

Deleting a goal in the app deletes it and all of its entries. Uninstalling the
app deletes all of its data from your device.

## Children

GoalStat is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if advertising is added), the updated
version will be posted at this address with a new "Last updated" date.
