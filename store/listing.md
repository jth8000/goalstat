# Google Play listing (draft)

**App name** (30 max): GoalStat: Goal & Habit Tracker

**Short description** (80 max):
Track daily, weekly, and monthly goals with streaks, pace, and simple stats.

**Full description** (4000 max):

GoalStat is a simple, private tracker for the goals and habits you care about.

Log each day in seconds: flip a toggle for yes/no goals ("Read", "Meditated")
or enter a number for measurable ones ("Cardio: 45 min", "Water: 64 oz").

Evaluate goals the way they actually work:
• Daily goals, like "Cardio ≥ 30 min"
• Weekly goals, like "Socialize ≥ 3 hrs a week" or "Eat out ≤ 2 times a week"
• Monthly goals, like "Read ≥ 20 days a month"
• Things to avoid, like "No smoking"

Know where you stand mid-week or mid-month. GoalStat tells you whether you're
on target, behind pace, or can no longer make it, and by how much.

See your progress:
• Streaks in days, weeks, or months
• On-target percentages over recent periods
• Charts for every goal
• A full history, exportable to CSV

Forgot to log yesterday? Pick any past day from the calendar; days you've
already logged are checked off.

Private by design: no account, no sign-up, and your data never leaves your
phone unless you export it.
